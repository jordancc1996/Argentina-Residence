Decree 524/2025 assigns a relevant investment to the Ministry of Economy and tells APCI to issue complementary rules. Those rules, and any dollar amount, were not found as enacted text. The decrees do not set a confirmed minimum amount. The [program status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page records that the pathway should not be treated as an open application program unless the government publishes the applicable criteria and confirms that applications are being accepted. Timing is on the [launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) page, and that date is not confirmed.

## What's expected next

No enacted minimum amount was located. The decrees do not identify real estate, a business, or a fund as a qualifying or disqualifying investment. Documentation standards for foreign applicants remain unpublished. The [Argentina citizenship by investment launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) note records that procedures and due diligence protocols were still undefined in the published decrees. This page does not treat a tender cancellation as an established fact.

Serious investors are advised to begin organizing source-of-funds documentation and consulting with qualified counsel before any filing channel exists.
