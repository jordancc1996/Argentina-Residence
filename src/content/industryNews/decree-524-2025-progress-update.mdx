Decree 524/2025 assigns a relevant investment to the Ministry of Economy and tells APCI to issue complementary rules. Those rules, and any dollar amount, were not found as enacted text. Secondary reports have described $500,000 and $1,000,000. Those figures are not current qualifying requirements. The [program status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page records that APCI is not processing applications. Timing is on the [launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) page, and that date is not confirmed.

## What's expected next

No enacted structure of $500,000 or $1,000,000 was located. The decree does not name real estate, a business, or a fund. Documentation standards for foreign applicants remain unpublished. The [Argentina citizenship by investment launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) note records that procedures and due diligence protocols were still undefined after the April 2026 tender cancellation.

Serious investors are advised to begin organizing source-of-funds documentation and consulting with qualified counsel before any filing channel exists.
