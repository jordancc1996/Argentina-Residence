Decree 524/2025 assigns a relevant investment to the Ministry of Economy and tells APCI to issue complementary rules. The [decree text](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) itself does not set a dollar amount. A later [Ministry of Economy announcement on October 2, 2026](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/luis-caputo-anuncio-la-puesta-en-marcha-del-programa-de-ciudadania-por-inversion-de) stated a $350,000 National Treasury contribution or an $800,000 public-bond subscription. That announcement is separate from the decree. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open. The government expects the application process to become operational during Q4 2026. No exact filing date has been announced. The [program status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page is the current snapshot. The [October 2 announcement](/press/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-q4-2026) is the first-party summary. Timing is on the [launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) page.

## What's expected next

Decree 524/2025 does not identify real estate, a business, or a fund as a qualifying or disqualifying investment, and the decree itself does not set a dollar amount. The October 2, 2026 announcement later stated the $350,000 Treasury contribution and the $800,000 public-bond route. Documentation standards for foreign applicants remain unpublished. The [Argentina citizenship by investment launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) note records that an exact filing date has not been announced. This page does not treat a tender cancellation as an established fact.

Serious investors are advised to begin organizing source-of-funds documentation and consulting with qualified counsel before any filing channel exists.
