Argentina citizenship by investment vs Portugal Golden Visa compares Argentina's announced citizenship routes with Portugal's live residency-by-investment program. Government applications in Argentina are not yet confirmed as open. As European nations restrict their residency programs, South America is emerging as a competitive alternative.

## The Structural Difference: Citizenship versus Residency

<ComparisonBlock id="portugal" />

Argentina's program, as outlined in Decree 524/2025, is a direct citizenship pathway. For that structure without the Portugal comparison, see the [program guide](/guides/argentina-golden-visa-program). If a file were later approved, the described result would be Argentine citizenship without a prior residency period. The legal framework does not yet confirm a processing calendar. Processing time is unknown. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open.

Conversely, the Portugal Golden Visa is a residency by investment program. It grants a residence permit that allows the holder to live and work in Portugal and travel freely within the Schengen Area. To maintain this residency, AIMA requires at least 7 days in Portugal in the first year and at least 14 days in each later year. Citizenship is not immediate. [Organic Law 1/2026](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei-organica/1-2026-1123539996), in force on 19 May 2026, requires ten years of legal residence for nationals of countries other than Portuguese-speaking countries and European Union member states, and seven years for those two groups. The applicant must show sufficient knowledge of the Portuguese language, culture, history, and national symbols. The Justice Ministry states that the new rules apply to applications filed after entry into force, and that pending files keep the previous text. A Golden Visa is a residence permit. It does not itself confer Portuguese nationality. See the [Ministry of Justice note](https://justica.gov.pt/Noticias/Lei-da-Nacionalidade-novas-regras-entram-em-vigor-a-19-de-maio).

## Investment Thresholds and Qualifying Assets

The Portugal Golden Visa fund route cited here is a capital transfer of at least [€500,000 into qualifying non-real-estate investment funds](https://aima.gov.pt/pt/viver/autorizacao-de-residencia-para-investimento-art-90-o-a), as stated by AIMA. The [October 2, 2026 announcement](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/luis-caputo-anuncio-la-puesta-en-marcha-del-programa-de-ciudadania-por-inversion-de) states a $350,000 National Treasury contribution or an $800,000 public-bond subscription for Argentina. See the [investment requirements](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-requirements).

AIMA states that the investment activity cannot be destined, directly or indirectly, to real estate investment. Other listed routes include research, company capital with jobs, and cultural or scientific investment. Those routes are not restated here as a complete menu.

The decrees do not identify real estate, a business, or a fund as a qualifying or disqualifying investment. This page does not state a tender cancellation as an established fact. Gazette publication of Resolution 522/2026 was not located.

## Global Mobility and Passport Strength

The Portuguese residence permit allows the holder to live and work in Portugal and to travel within the Schengen Area. It is not a passport for travel outside Europe. Portuguese citizenship is a later nationality application. [Organic Law 1/2026](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei-organica/1-2026-1123539996), in force on 19 May 2026, requires ten years of legal residence for nationals of countries other than Portuguese-speaking countries and European Union member states, and seven years for those two groups. It is not a five-year citizenship clock for either category. A Portuguese passport, if one is later issued, is a separate document. Portugal is on the [U.S. Visa Waiver Program](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visa-waiver-program.html) list, so Portuguese citizens can seek visa-free travel to the United States under that program. This page does not state a destination count for the Portuguese passport.

An Argentine passport, if one were later issued, is scored by the [Henley Passport Index](https://cdn.henleyglobal.com/storage/app/media/HPI/Henley%20Passport%20Index%202026%20April%20Global%20Ranking.pdf) of 13 April 2026 at rank 15, with a visa-free score of 168. That score includes visa on arrival and electronic travel authorization. It is not a residency right, and that passport is not being issued through an open government application. See [visa-free travel](/faq/argentina-visa-free-travel). Argentine citizenship, if granted, is also described as freedom of movement and the right to work in Mercosur member states, including Brazil, Uruguay, and Paraguay, under the [MERCOSUR residence framework](https://www.mercosur.int/logro-14-residencia-legal-en-cualquier-pais-del-bloque). Argentina is not currently in the [U.S. Visa Waiver Program](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visa-waiver-program.html). A later request to rejoin that program is not a confirmed travel right.

## Family Inclusion and Generational Planning

The Portugal Golden Visa allows the main applicant to include a spouse, dependent children, and dependent parents. Portuguese nationality, if later acquired under the residence period in Organic Law 1/2026, is a separate legal status from the Golden Visa.

The decrees do not establish a general family-inclusion rule for investor applicants. An Argentine passport is not a benefit of residence or of an uncompleted investment-naturalization application. This page does not state a family waiting period, or the absence of one, as an enacted Argentine rule.

## Conclusion

Portugal is a live residence path with a later citizenship clock. Argentina's provision is investment naturalization, distinct from residence. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open. Portugal remains a residence permit with a later nationality application under Organic Law 1/2026. That law sets seven years of legal residence for nationals of Portuguese-speaking countries and European Union member states, and ten years for other nationals. It is not a five-year citizenship clock. The other operating European residence-permit comparison on this site is [Argentina citizenship by investment vs Greece Golden Visa](/guides/argentina-citizenship-investment-vs-greece-golden-visa). An Argentine passport is not a benefit of residence or of an investment-naturalization application. [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) gives DNM 30 business days to decide after DNM receives APCI's report. That period is not total or guaranteed processing time.
