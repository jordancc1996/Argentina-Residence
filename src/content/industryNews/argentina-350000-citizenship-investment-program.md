The Argentina $350,000 Citizenship Investment Program is planned for launch in the fourth quarter of 2026. The program was announced in Paris on [October 2, 2026](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/luis-caputo-anuncio-la-puesta-en-marcha-del-programa-de-ciudadania-por-inversion-de). It states a non-refundable $350,000 contribution for one applicant or an $800,000 investment in government bonds. The bond's final terms are not public as of this analysis. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open. The [current program status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page is the snapshot, and the [expected launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) has no exact filing day.

## Two Routes With Different Costs

Under the treasury route, the principal applicant pays at least $350,000 to the national treasury, and the contribution is non-refundable.

Under the bond route, the principal applicant purchases an $800,000 government security created for the program. The bond covers only the principal applicant. Dependents require additional treasury contributions under either route. Those announced routes are set out on the [investment requirements](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-requirements) page.

The government's reported plan calls for a seven-year, non-interest-bearing bond. Final terms remain unpublished. Applicants therefore do not yet have complete information regarding the bond's liquidity, risks, repayment terms, or other material conditions.

## Family Costs

A spouse, eligible children, or both can join the principal applicant if the required family relationship is established and the applicable additional treasury contribution is paid:

- Spouse: $100,000
- Unmarried children ages 18 to 25: $100,000
- Children under age 18: $25,000

Adult children ages 18 to 25 must never have been married. The child does not need to demonstrate financial dependence. Final application rules still need to clarify when and how an applicant child's age will be assessed. The [family members](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-family) page records the same announced contribution amounts.

For a family consisting of one principal applicant, one spouse, and two children under age 18, the treasury route would cost $500,000. This consists of the principal applicant's $350,000 contribution, the spouse's $100,000 contribution, and $50,000 for the two children.

For the same family, the bond route would require $950,000. This consists of the $800,000 bond investment plus $150,000 in non-refundable contributions for the spouse and two children.

## Applicant Checks and Decisions

The Agency for Citizenship by Investment Programs (APCI) will lead the review process and work with relevant government agencies to assess applicants, including:

- The State Intelligence Secretariat
- The Financial Information Unit
- The Ministry of Security
- The Ministry of the Interior

The review will cover:

- Applicant identity
- Lawful source and path of funds
- Assets and financial background
- Country risk
- Criminal records and reputational concerns
- Immigration history

APCI will send its recommendation to the National Directorate of Migration, which will approve or reject each application.
