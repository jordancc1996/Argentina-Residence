**BUENOS AIRES, Argentina - October 2, 2026 -** **Argentina Citizenship by Investment Q4 2026** plans now include newly announced financial terms for foreign applicants and their families, providing additional details on the anticipated opening of the program.

The [announcement](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/luis-caputo-anuncio-la-puesta-en-marcha-del-programa-de-ciudadania-por-inversion-de) establishes two routes for principal applicants: a **$350,000 non-refundable contribution to Argentina's National Treasury** or an **$800,000 subscription to a public bond** created specifically for the program.

Argentina Residence, an independent advisory resource covering Argentine residency, citizenship, and investment migration, is reviewing the announcement and updating its research as additional implementation details become available.

The latest research on the [Argentina Citizenship By Investment](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) program is available at ArgentinaResidence.com.

## Argentina sets investment thresholds

The announcement provides the first official dollar thresholds for Argentina's investment-based citizenship framework.

Under the announced structure, a principal applicant may qualify through one of two financial routes:

- A $350,000 non-refundable contribution to the National Treasury
- An $800,000 subscription to a public bond created specifically for the program

This development addresses one of the major outstanding questions surrounding the program. Argentina's earlier legal framework established an investment-based route to naturalization without specifying the minimum investment amount or exact form a qualifying investment would take.

Decree 366/2025 established a naturalization pathway for foreign nationals who demonstrate a qualifying relevant investment in Argentina, regardless of the duration of their residence. Decree 524/2025 subsequently established additional administrative procedures for evaluating those applications.

The October 2 announcement adds the financial thresholds that had previously been absent from the published framework.

## Family contribution requirements announced

Argentina also announced additional contribution requirements for immediate family members included in an application.

A spouse requires an additional **$100,000 contribution** to the National Treasury.

Children between 18 and 25 require a **$100,000 contribution** and must satisfy the conditions established for that category, including being unmarried and without children of their own.

Children under 18 require an additional **$25,000 contribution**.

The government provides the example of a principal applicant applying with a spouse and two children under 18. Under the contribution route, the combined amount would be **$500,000**.

Family contributions are structured as payments to the National Treasury rather than additional subscriptions to the public-bond route.

## Argentina Citizenship by Investment applications expected to open in Q4 2026

The announcement provides financial terms for the program, but prospective applicants should distinguish between an announced program and an open government filing channel.

Argentina's government states that the program is expected to become operational for receiving applications during the **fourth quarter of 2026**.

Until the filing process becomes operational, prospective investors should continue to treat the program as an announced pathway rather than an application that can currently be submitted.

Additional implementation details may also be published as the government moves toward accepting applications.

Argentina Residence will continue monitoring official government publications and updating its program research as those details become available.

## How the Argentina Citizenship By Investment process is expected to work

Argentina has already established part of the administrative framework through Decree 524/2025.

The Agency for Citizenship by Investment Programs is responsible for evaluating whether an applicant's investment qualifies under the applicable framework and conducting the required review process.

The framework includes participation by Argentine authorities responsible for immigration, security, financial intelligence, criminal records, and identity verification.

Following the Agency's evaluation, its report is submitted to the National Directorate of Migration.

Existing regulations provide the National Directorate of Migration with **30 business days** to issue a reasoned decision after receiving the Agency's report. This period should not be interpreted as a 30-business-day total processing time for the entire Citizenship by Investment application.

The government has also stated that program funds must pass through the formal financial system and comply with applicable financial-transparency, anti-money-laundering, and counter-terrorist-financing requirements.

## What the October 2026 announcement changes

Until now, several important elements of the Argentina Citizenship By Investment framework remained unresolved.

The underlying legislation created the possibility of naturalization based on a relevant investment, but the government had not publicly established a minimum investment threshold or confirmed the financial instruments that would qualify.

The October 2 announcement provides answers to several of those questions by establishing the **$350,000 Treasury contribution**, the **$800,000 public-bond route**, and additional contribution amounts for qualifying family members.

The next significant development will be the opening of the government application process and publication of any additional procedural requirements.
