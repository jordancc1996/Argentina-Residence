# Can I travel visa-free with Argentine residency?

No. Argentine residency is not an Argentine passport. Visa-free travel follows the passport the traveler already holds, not a residence permit.

An Argentine passport is a separate document. The [Henley Passport Index](https://cdn.henleyglobal.com/storage/app/media/HPI/Henley%20Passport%20Index%202026%20April%20Global%20Ranking.pdf) edition dated 13 April 2026 ranks it 15th, with a visa-free score of 168. Henley counts destinations that do not require a prior visa. The score includes visa-free entry, visa on arrival, and an electronic travel authorization. It is not a count of visa-free entry alone, and it is not a right that comes with Argentine residency. The proposed citizenship-by-investment framework is not open, so it is not currently issuing that passport. See [program status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status).
