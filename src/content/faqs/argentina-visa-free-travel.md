# Can I travel visa-free with Argentine residency?

No. Argentine residency is not an Argentine passport. Visa-free travel follows the passport the traveler already holds, not a residence permit.

An Argentine passport is a separate document. The [Henley Passport Index](https://cdn.henleyglobal.com/storage/app/media/HPI/Henley%20Passport%20Index%202026%20April%20Global%20Ranking.pdf) edition dated 13 April 2026 ranks it 15th, with a visa-free score of 168. Henley counts destinations that do not require a prior visa. The score includes visa-free entry, visa on arrival, and an electronic travel authorization. It is not a count of visa-free entry alone. It is not a right that comes with Argentine residency. It is not a benefit of an investment-naturalization application. The pathway should not be treated as an open application program unless the government publishes the applicable criteria and confirms that applications are being accepted.
