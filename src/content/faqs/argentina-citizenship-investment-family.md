# Argentina Residency Family Members - Spouse and Dependents

For the proposed Argentina citizenship-by-investment framework, a spouse and children under 18 are expected on the same application. The status of dependents 18 and older is unknown. Neither point has been officially confirmed. Government applications are not open. This is not a description of a residence permit that can be filed today. The longer note is in the [Argentina Golden Visa program](/guides/argentina-golden-visa-program) guide. Amounts are on the [investment requirements](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-requirements) page.

Each included person is expected to need their own documents. Whether extra investment applies for family members has not been published.
