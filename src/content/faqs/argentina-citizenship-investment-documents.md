# Argentina Golden Visa Required Documents - Apostille and Translations

Expected Argentina Golden Visa documents include a valid passport, apostilled civil records, a criminal background check, proof of funds, and Spanish translations. This is not a published APCI checklist. Government applications are not open. Those items sit inside the expected [due diligence process](/guides/argentina-citizenship-investment-due-diligence) file, once APCI publishes protocols.
