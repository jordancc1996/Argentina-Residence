# What is the Argentina Golden Visa program?

The Argentina Golden Visa is a proposed citizenship-by-investment framework under [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731). It is not an open residency visa. Government applications are not currently open. Current availability is on the [citizenship by investment status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page. The [Argentina Golden Visa program guide](/guides/argentina-golden-visa-program) is the longer explanation.
