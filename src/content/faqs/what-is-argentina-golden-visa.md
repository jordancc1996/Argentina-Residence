# What is the Argentina Golden Visa program?

Argentine law contains a naturalization pathway for a foreign national who makes an investment that the Ministry of Economy defines as relevant. The October 2, 2026 announcement states a $350,000 National Treasury contribution or an $800,000 public-bond subscription. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open.

DNU 366/2025 created the Agencia de Programas de Ciudadanía por Inversión (APCI) within the amended citizenship framework. [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) subsequently established a basic application and review procedure involving APCI. The investment-naturalization provision is distinct from temporary or permanent residence. It is not a residence permit and it is not a passport.

Current wording is on the [citizenship by investment status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page. The [October 2 announcement](/press/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-q4-2026) is the dated summary of the financial terms. The [Argentina Golden Visa program guide](/guides/argentina-golden-visa-program) is the longer explanation.
