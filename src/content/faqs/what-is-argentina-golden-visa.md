# What is the Argentina Golden Visa program?

Argentine law contains a naturalization pathway for a foreign national who makes an investment that the Ministry of Economy defines as relevant. The decrees do not set a confirmed minimum amount or identify qualifying investment types. The pathway should not be treated as an open application program unless the government publishes the applicable criteria and confirms that applications are being accepted.

DNU 366/2025 created the Agencia de Programas de Ciudadanía por Inversión (APCI) within the amended citizenship framework. [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) subsequently established a basic application and review procedure involving APCI. The investment-naturalization provision is distinct from temporary or permanent residence. It is not a residence permit and it is not a passport.

Current wording is on the [citizenship by investment status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page. The [Argentina Golden Visa program guide](/guides/argentina-golden-visa-program) is the longer explanation.
