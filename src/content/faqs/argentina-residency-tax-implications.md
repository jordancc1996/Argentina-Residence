# What are the tax implications?

Immigration residence, citizenship, and Argentine tax residence are different questions. This page does not state an Argentine tax rate or a treaty list.

[Law 27.802](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-27802-423680/texto), sanctioned on 27 February 2026 and published on 6 March 2026, adds a paragraph to article 116 of the Income Tax Law. A foreign person who obtains Argentine citizenship by naturalization because of a relevant investment, under Law 346 article 2, is not a tax resident under article 116(a) solely because of that naturalization. For article 116(b), that person is still treated as a foreign national. A person who was already a permanent resident when the citizenship was granted remains a tax resident. The statute does not say that citizenship prevents tax residence if another legal test is met. Government applications for the investment framework are not open. This is not personal tax advice.
