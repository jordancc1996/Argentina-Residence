Argentina $800,000 CBI bond is the October 2 alternative to the proposed $350,000 non-refundable Treasury contribution. An applicant may subscribe to a public bond created specifically for the Citizenship by Investment Program. That is the extent of the capital detail the announcement gives. It does not state when the bond matures, how or when principal can be redeemed, whether it pays interest, or whether an investor may sell or transfer it. Buyers should not treat the announced amount as a promise of repayment or a guaranteed return.

Status checked November 11, 2026.

The [Ministry of Economy announcement](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/luis-caputo-anuncio-la-puesta-en-marcha-del-programa-de-ciudadania-por-inversion-de) says the bond route is a subscription, and that funds must move through the formal financial system. It does not provide a prospectus, final issuance terms, payment instructions, or the identity and mechanics of a settlement or custody provider. Until those documents are published, the exact legal instrument and investor's rights remain unknown.

## What repayment and maturity are established?

Nothing in the announcement states a maturity date, redemption schedule, early-repayment right, put option, or repayment guarantee. A bond is commonly understood as debt, but its label alone does not establish when this particular security must repay principal, what entity owes the debt, or what remedies apply after a default. The announcement's statement that proceeds will strengthen Argentina's fiscal and financial position describes the intended use of funds, not a repayment commitment to each subscriber.

The governing procedural framework does not fill that gap. [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) sets out how the Citizenship by Investment Agency evaluates an application and sends a recommendation to the National Directorate of Migration. It directs the Agency to issue additional procedural rules, but does not set this bond's maturity, redemption, or yield. The statutory framework for determining qualifying investments likewise does not substitute for the security's final issuance documents.

## Is there a yield, and can the bond be sold?

No coupon, interest rate, indexation formula, currency-conversion provision, or yield-to-maturity appears in the October announcement. Do not infer that the $800,000 subscription earns interest, or estimate returns as though a rate had been published. Whether the instrument is fixed-rate, zero-coupon, or structured another way is not confirmed.

Transferability is also unresolved. The announcement does not say whether an investor may sell the bond, transfer it to a family member, pledge it as collateral, or move it between accounts. It does not identify an exchange listing or secondary market. Any citizenship-related holding period or rule on keeping the investment after approval is also unknown. Ask for the final terms to spell out restrictions, transfer procedures, and what happens if an application is refused or withdrawn. The announced subscription does not by itself answer whether funds are returned in those cases.

## Custody, risks, and paperwork to request

No custodian, broker, registrar, depository, or account-opening process is named in the announcement. Before sending funds, a prospective subscriber should obtain official issuance documents from the competent Argentine authorities and confirm how the security is recorded in the investor's name, who holds it, how ownership is evidenced, and how statements and redemption payments are delivered. Do not rely on a private intermediary's summary as a substitute for government instructions.

The investment also carries risks beyond the possibility of a delayed or missed payment. Argentina's fiscal and market conditions can affect sovereign debt values. Currency, transfer, liquidity, legal, and operational risks may matter, depending on final terms and the investor's home-country rules. A bond's market value before maturity can fall even if it promises principal repayment at a future date. These are general bond risks, not claims about terms that Argentina has yet to disclose.

Request the final prospectus or equivalent offering document, the instrument's legal authorization, subscription and settlement instructions, custody arrangements, fees, tax treatment information, and written procedures for refusal, withdrawal, transfer, and redemption. Confirm that the terms you receive match official publications. The [program requirements page](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-requirements) and [October announcement overview](/press/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-q4-2026) track the announced route, while Reuters reported the announced US$800,000 bond and anticipated Q4 2026 application window. Neither replaces final bond documentation. The government has said applications are expected to become operational during Q4 2026. As of November 11, an open application channel and final bond terms should not be assumed.
