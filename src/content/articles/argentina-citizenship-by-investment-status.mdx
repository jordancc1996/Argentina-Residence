## Current legal status

The decrees that describe the pathway are still on the books. The June 2026 rulings and the pending Supreme Court appeal are the unresolved legal question. No launch date can be confirmed while this is unresolved. The full legal narrative, tender record, and source notes are on the [Argentina citizenship by investment launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) article. This page does not repeat that account.

## What this means for prospective investors

There is no filing portal and no confirmed investment amount. Two paths are currently anticipated, subject to final regulation: a non-refundable $500,000 USD Treasury contribution, or a $1,000,000 USD 7-year government bond. Real estate, business or startup ventures, and investment funds are not anticipated qualifying paths. Neither path has been officially confirmed.

Work that can be done now is record-keeping and counsel, not an APCI submission. Do not treat this snapshot as an application checklist.
