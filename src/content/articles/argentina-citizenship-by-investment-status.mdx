## Current legal status

Decree 366/2025 and Decree 524/2025 remain published in the Boletín Oficial. On 30 June 2026 the [National Electoral Chamber judgment](https://scw.pjn.gov.ar/scw/viewer.seam?id=Mqesj2eOmAQBabgt1%2FDrEI4ir7gxNA6EjeNi34VAPO0%3D&tipoDoc=sentencia) declared Decree 366/2025 null. The executive appealed. That appeal is the unresolved legal question. The judgment does not say that a citizenship-by-investment program has been cancelled as a final national result. No launch date can be confirmed while the appeal is unresolved. The tender record is on the [launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) article.

## What this means for prospective investors

There is no filing portal and no enacted investment amount. Decree 524/2025 does not set a dollar figure. Secondary reports have described a $500,000 USD Treasury contribution and a $1,000,000 USD 7-year bond. Those figures were not found in an enacted regulation. They are not current qualifying requirements.

Work that can be done now is record-keeping and counsel, not an APCI submission. Do not treat this snapshot as an application checklist.

## Sources and methodology

This page reflects only decrees, resolutions, and court rulings, updated when a development is confirmed. Future updates require a manual edit of this file and of its last-updated date. It does not estimate outcomes or project a filing window.

The framework texts are [Decree 366/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/326096/20250529) and [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) in the Boletín Oficial. Decree 524/2025 does not state a dollar amount. It tells the Ministry of Economy to define a relevant investment, and it tells APCI to issue complementary procedure rules.
