## Current legal status

Decree 366/2025 and Decree 524/2025 remain published in the Boletín Oficial. Two federal appellate decisions in June 2026, including the [National Electoral Chamber judgment in Yang, Liping](https://scw.pjn.gov.ar/scw/viewer.seam?id=Mqesj2eOmAQBabgt1%2FDrEI4ir7gxNA6EjeNi34VAPO0%3D&tipoDoc=sentencia), questioned or invalidated aspects of DNU 366/2025 in individual naturalization cases. Neither concerned a CBI applicant. The decisions create uncertainty about the DNU provisions on which the investment pathway depends. The available rulings do not establish the final, nationwide effect of the decisions. This page does not state that a Supreme Court appeal is pending. Timing notes are on the [launch date](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date) article.

## What this means for prospective investors

No confirmed government filing portal is open. Decree 524/2025 does not set a dollar figure. The [October 2, 2026 announcement](/press/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-q4-2026) later stated a $350,000 non-refundable National Treasury contribution or an $800,000 public-bond subscription. A government example for a principal applicant, a spouse, and two children under 18 on the contribution route is $500,000. Family add-ons in that example are Treasury contributions: $100,000 for a spouse and $25,000 for each child under 18. A qualifying child age 18 to 25 has an announced additional $100,000 contribution, subject to the announced conditions for that category, including being unmarried and without children of their own. These announced terms are not a guarantee of approval. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open.

Work that can be done now is record-keeping and counsel, not an APCI submission. Do not treat this snapshot as an application checklist.

## Sources and methodology

This page records decrees, resolutions, court rulings, and the October 2, 2026 government announcement, updated when a development is confirmed. It does not convert the expected Q4 2026 window into an exact filing date.

The framework texts are [Decree 366/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/326096/20250529) and [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) in the Boletín Oficial. Decree 524/2025 does not state a dollar amount. It tells the Ministry of Economy to define a relevant investment, and it tells APCI to issue complementary procedure rules. The later [Ministry of Economy announcement of October 2, 2026](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/luis-caputo-anuncio-la-puesta-en-marcha-del-programa-de-ciudadania-por-inversion-de) stated the Treasury and public-bond routes. The [October 2 announcement](/press/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-q4-2026) is this site's summary of that communiqué.
