Argentina CBI legal stability is not settled by two appellate rulings in 2026. They do not, by themselves, establish that the investor pathway is invalid or that citizenship already granted through it is void. The cases involved ordinary naturalization applicants, not investors. Their reasoning may still matter because both routes sit within changes made by Decree of Necessity and Urgency 366/2025 (DNU 366/2025).

Status checked November 11, 2026.

## What the courts decided

DNU 366/2025 amended Citizenship Law 346. Among other changes, it introduced naturalization for a foreigner who makes a "relevant investment," regardless of residence duration, and transferred naturalization decisions from federal courts to the National Directorate of Migration (DNM). The [official decree text](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/norma-413297/texto) sets out those amendments.

On June 18, Sala III of the Federal Civil and Commercial Court of Appeals ruled in an ordinary naturalization case that challenged the transfer to DNM. As detailed in [specialist legal reporting](https://ninolegal.com/novedad/inconstitucionalidad-dnu-366-2025-ciudadania-migraciones), the panel questioned the executive's use of an emergency decree and the removal of procedural safeguards. It sent the matter back for judicial handling. On June 30, the National Electoral Chamber ruled in the separate *Yang, Liping* case, also an ordinary naturalization dispute. Its [published judgment](https://scw.pjn.gov.ar/scw/viewer.seam?id=Mqesj2eOmAQBabgt1%2FDrEI4ir7gxNA6EjeNi34VAPO0%3D&tipoDoc=sentencia) addressed the decree's constitutional limits in that applicant's case and ordered a citizenship outcome for him. Neither case concerned an investment, APCI review, or a CBI application.

That distinction limits what can be concluded. Both decisions were made in individual disputes. They were not a general cancellation of DNU 366/2025, and neither specifically adjudicated the investor provision. The [IMI Daily account](https://www.imidaily.com/latin-america/court-faults-mileis-use-of-a-decree-to-overhaul-citizenship-in-a-case-specific-ruling/) likewise distinguishes the narrow case outcome from the broader questions its reasoning raises. The rulings therefore create legal uncertainty, not a settled answer on CBI.

## Why investors could still be affected

The investor route depends on the amendments to Law 346 in the same decree. Its implementing [Decree 524/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/329061/20250731) says a foreigner with a relevant investment may apply through the Agency for Citizenship by Investment Programs (APCI). APCI assesses the investment and gathers agency reviews, then recommends approval or rejection to DNM. DNM is assigned 30 business days after receiving APCI's report to issue a reasoned decision.

The appellate cases did not test whether the investment provisions independently satisfy constitutional requirements. A future court could examine their connection to the broader amendments, the decree's stated necessity and urgency, and the administrative process. That is a possible legal argument, not a prediction. No CBI-specific appellate ruling has resolved those issues.

## Three situations, three different questions

**A hypothetical pending application:** An applicant could face uncertainty about which authority has jurisdiction, how the DNU's validity applies, or whether the case can proceed while the framework is reviewed. There is no confirmed government filing channel open as of this date, so this is a scenario, not a description of a live CBI caseload. The government's [program status information](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) also says applications are not yet confirmed open. The appellate cases do not direct APCI or DNM to stop processing investor cases.

**An approval not yet finalized:** DNM decides an investment application after APCI's assessment. The effect of any decision and available review would depend on its terms, the procedure followed, and later court rulings. The judgments do not automatically reverse an approval. An applicant should obtain advice on the record, rather than assume an approval is immune or void.

**Citizenship already granted:** The cases contain no general order cancelling citizenship previously granted to investors. Whether a final grant could be challenged, and on what grounds or when, is a separate question these rulings do not answer. A decree being invalid in one case does not prove a person's citizenship has been revoked.

## What is known, and what is not

The decree and implementing rules remain published, while appellate decisions have challenged aspects of the citizenship changes in individual cases. Their reach for investors is unresolved. Argentina Residence's [status page](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) notes applications are not confirmed open. A published regulation is not a court-confirmed guarantee of legal security.
