## Proposed program, not an open filing channel

**Argentina citizenship by investment for Americans is proposed under Decree 524/2025; [the pathway should not be treated as an open application program](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status).** The proposed citizenship-by-investment framework is already attracting considerable attention from high-net-worth individuals and investors interested in diversifying their global mobility footprint in South America.

## Legal Basis and Program Status

The **Argentinian citizenship by investment program** was established through an executive order that amended Argentina's nationality law and created a formal pathway for foreign investors to obtain citizenship. [DNU 366/2025](https://www.boletinoficial.gob.ar/detalleAviso/primera/326096/20250529) added naturalization for a relevant investment. That provision is distinct from ordinary naturalization. The decrees do not specify a physical-presence rule for investor applicants. The [October 2, 2026 announcement](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/luis-caputo-anuncio-la-puesta-en-marcha-del-programa-de-ciudadania-por-inversion-de) later stated a $350,000 National Treasury contribution or an $800,000 public-bond subscription. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open. See the [citizenship by investment status](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-status) page.

Argentina's citizenship by investment program is designed to attract foreign investment into the Argentine economy.

An existing Argentine passport is a separate document from residence. It is not a benefit of an investment-naturalization application. The Henley score below is a passport snapshot, not a residency right.

This can be extremely beneficial for American business owners and families because it grants access to the entire South American continent as a potential base of operations or residence. Argentine nationals are eligible to apply for the U.S. E-2 Treaty Investor visa because Argentina is an E-2 treaty country. The [U.S. Department of State Treaty Countries list](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/fees/treaty.html) lists Argentina for E-1 and E-2, with a treaty effective date of December 20, 1854. Obtaining Argentine citizenship does not by itself qualify someone for an E-2 visa. Applicants must separately satisfy the applicable E-2 investment and eligibility requirements. [Law 27,802](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/normativa/nacional/ley-27802-423680/texto), Article 194, provides that naturalization under the relevant-investment provision does not, by itself, create Argentine tax residence. Tax residence can still arise under the applicable Argentine rules and the person's circumstances, including time spent in Argentina. Citizenship is not an exemption from Argentine tax, and it is not permanent protection from taxation of worldwide income.

### U.S. Visa Waiver Program

Towards the end of July 2025 President Trump and Argentine President Javier Milei met and announced that they wanted to prepare a way for Argentinian passport holders to enter the United States without a visa. The [Visa Waiver Program](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visa-waiver-program.html) allows citizens of the countries designated on that State Department page to travel into the United States and stay for up to 90 days without a visa. The number of designated countries can change, so this page does not state a fixed count.

The only Latin American country currently in the Visa Waiver Program is Chile. While this is currently speculation, the addition of Argentina into the Visa Waiver program would make the citizenship-by-investment route more relevant to potential investors.

## Before published regulations

With this being a new program, investors should proceed with caution and talk to many advisors to fully understand the scope of the investment and what is expected. The program was introduced by executive decree and while the government has embedded it directly into the nationality law there still could be unforeseen challenges for Argentina to fully enact the law and open the citizenship-by-investment program.

**Investors are strongly advised to wait until the final, detailed regulations are officially published before making any financial commitments.**
