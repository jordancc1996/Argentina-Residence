Argentina citizenship by investment vs the Greece Golden Visa compares a proposed direct citizenship framework with an established European residence-by-investment program.

Decree 524/2025 does not set a dollar amount. The October 2, 2026 announcement states a $350,000 National Treasury contribution or an $800,000 public-bond subscription. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open. The pathway should not be treated as an open application program.

The Greece Golden Visa is a residence permit, not citizenship. [Law 5100/2024](https://www.migration.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/%CE%9D%CF%8C%CE%BC%CE%BF%CF%82-5100_2024-%CE%BA%CF%89%CE%B4%CE%B9%CE%BA%CE%BF%CF%80%CE%BF%CE%B9%CE%B7%CE%BC%CE%AD%CE%BD%CE%BF%CF%82-%CE%BC%CE%B5-%CF%84%CE%BF%CE%BD-5167_2024-%CE%A6%CE%95%CE%9A-%CE%91-49_5.4.2024.pdf), as published by the Ministry of Migration and Asylum, sets €800,000 for Attica, the Thessaloniki regional unit, Mykonos, Thira (Santorini), and islands with more than 3,100 inhabitants. The threshold is €400,000 elsewhere. The purchase is in one property. Built property, or property with a building permit, needs at least 120 square meters of main space. A €250,000 threshold applies when main space is converted to a residence, including certain idle industrial buildings, and when the purchase is a listed building for restoration. It is not a general €250,000 option.

## How Does the Greece Golden Visa Work?

The Greece Golden Visa provides a five-year renewable residence permit for qualifying investors and included family members. The investment must generally remain in place for the residence permit to be renewed.

The permit allows the holder to reside in Greece and provides short-stay travel within the Schengen Area. It does not provide Greek citizenship or a Greek passport.

## How Much Do You Need to Invest in Greek Real Estate?

Greece uses location-based real estate investment thresholds.

Law 5100/2024 sets €800,000 in Attica, the Thessaloniki regional unit, Mykonos, Thira, and islands above 3,100 inhabitants, and €400,000 elsewhere. The investment is in one property, with a 120 square meter main-space rule for built property. The €250,000 figure is limited to the conversion and listed-building cases in that article.

## Does the Greece Golden Visa Require You to Live in Greece?

The provisions of Law 5100/2024 reviewed here set investment thresholds. They do not state a day-count for keeping the residence permit. Keeping that permit is not the same thing as qualifying for Greek citizenship.

## Does the Greece Golden Visa Lead to Citizenship?

Greek citizenship requires a separate naturalization process. The Golden Visa itself does not provide nationality.

This page does not state a Greek naturalization year count. That count was not taken from Law 5100/2024. Holding the residence permit does not itself confer Greek nationality.

## Is Argentina Citizenship by Investment Open?

No. Argentina is not currently accepting citizenship-by-investment applications.

The proposed framework is intended to provide Argentine nationality through qualifying investment rather than a renewable residence permit. Final application procedures, due-diligence protocols, processing times, and operational rules remain pending.

## Argentina vs Greece: What Is the Main Difference?

Greece offers an operating residence-by-investment program, with real estate thresholds generally ranging from €400,000 to €800,000 and a €250,000 route for defined exceptions. Investors receive a renewable residence permit, not immediate citizenship.

Argentina has published Decrees 366/2025 and 524/2025. Decree 524/2025 does not set a dollar amount. The October 2, 2026 announcement later stated a $350,000 National Treasury contribution or an $800,000 public-bond subscription. Government applications are not yet confirmed as open. Greece's figures above are residence thresholds, not Argentine citizenship thresholds.
