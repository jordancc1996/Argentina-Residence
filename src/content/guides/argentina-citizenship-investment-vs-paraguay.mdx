Argentina citizenship by investment vs Paraguay's Investor Pass compares a proposed direct citizenship framework with an operating permanent residency program.

Argentina's citizenship by investment framework is being developed as a direct route to Argentine citizenship through qualifying investment. Expected options include a $500,000 non-refundable contribution to the Argentine Treasury or a $1 million investment in a 7-year, 0% Argentine government bond, with the principal expected to be returned at maturity. No residence requirement is anticipated. Applications are not yet open, and final program terms have not been published.

Paraguay's Investor Pass provides permanent residency rather than direct citizenship. Qualifying options include a $70,000 commercial or industrial investment with at least five formal jobs, $200,000 in stock market holdings, $200,000 in qualifying real estate not intended for personal use, or $150,000 in tourism investment. Paraguay's Investor Pass is operating and applications are being processed.

## What Does Paraguay's Investor Pass Provide?

The Investor Pass provides permanent residence in Paraguay, not Paraguayan citizenship. Successful applicants receive a permanent-residence carnet, reported as valid for ten years, with authorization to work and conduct business in Paraguay.

Family members are not automatically included on the principal investor's application. Current program summaries state that dependents apply separately for temporary residence. After two years, the principal may be able to sponsor eligible family members for permanent residence.

## How Much Do You Need to Invest in Paraguay?

Paraguay's investment requirement depends on the Investor Pass category. The commercial and industrial route starts at $70,000 and requires at least five formal jobs. Stock market holdings require $200,000 and are generally subject to a minimum two-year hold.

Qualifying real estate requires at least $200,000 and cannot be intended for personal use. The tourism investment route requires at least $150,000.

## Does Paraguay Residency Lead to Citizenship?

Paraguay residency does not automatically provide citizenship. Naturalization is a separate process commonly described as potentially available after three years of legal residence, subject to language, integration, and other requirements. Approval is discretionary.

A Paraguayan residence card also does not itself provide the Mercosur mobility rights associated with Paraguayan nationality.

## Is Argentina Citizenship by Investment Open?

No. Argentina is not currently accepting citizenship-by-investment applications. Its proposed framework is intended to provide Argentine nationality through qualifying investment, rather than requiring investors to first obtain permanent residence and later pursue naturalization.

Final application procedures, due-diligence protocols, processing times, and operational rules remain pending.

## Argentina vs Paraguay: What Is the Main Difference?

Paraguay offers an operating investment route to permanent residence today, with qualifying investments starting at $70,000 depending on the category. Citizenship requires a later and separate naturalization process.

Argentina is developing a direct citizenship framework with substantially higher anticipated investment requirements. However, applications are not yet open and the expected terms remain subject to final implementation.
