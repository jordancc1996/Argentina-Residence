Argentina citizenship by investment vs Paraguay's Investor Pass compares a proposed direct citizenship framework with an operating permanent residency program.

Decree 524/2025 does not set a dollar amount for Argentina. The decrees do not set a confirmed minimum amount or identify qualifying investment types. The pathway should not be treated as an open application program.

Paraguay's investor route is permanent residence, not citizenship. [Migration Law 6984/2022](https://www.bacn.gov.py/leyes-paraguayas/10973/ley-n-6984-de-migraciones) excepts foreigners who demonstrate an investment under the SUACE statute, Law 4986/2013, from the usual temporary-residence step before permanent residence. The [National Migration Directorate](https://migraciones.gov.py/residencia-permanente-para-inversionistas-extranjeros-suace/) publishes that SUACE procedure. Dollar minimums of $70,000, $150,000, and $200,000 were not found in that statute or on that page, so they are not restated here. The investment does not itself create citizenship eligibility.

## What Does Paraguay's Investor Pass Provide?

The Investor Pass provides permanent residence in Paraguay, not Paraguayan citizenship. Successful applicants receive a permanent-residence carnet, reported as valid for ten years, with authorization to work and conduct business in Paraguay.

Family members are not automatically included on the principal investor's application. Current program summaries state that dependents apply separately for temporary residence. After two years, the principal may be able to sponsor eligible family members for permanent residence.

## How Much Do You Need to Invest in Paraguay?

Law 6984/2022 requires the investor to demonstrate the investment. It does not, in the text reviewed, set the dollar schedules previously repeated on this page. Those unsupported amounts have been removed.

## Does Paraguay Residency Lead to Citizenship?

Paraguay residency does not provide citizenship. The [Supreme Court naturalization page](https://www.pj.gov.py/contenido/463-carta-de-naturalizacion/463) requires three years of permanent residence, counted from the permanent-admission resolution, under Constitution article 148. Acordada 464/2007 treats that period as continuous residence with a real domicile, and it treats absences of more than three months in a year as a break. The applicant must also show regular work or a trade and good conduct. The investment itself does not create that eligibility.

A Paraguayan residence card also does not itself provide the Mercosur mobility rights associated with Paraguayan nationality.

## Is Argentina Citizenship by Investment Open?

No. Argentina is not currently accepting citizenship-by-investment applications. Its proposed framework is intended to provide Argentine nationality through qualifying investment, rather than requiring investors to first obtain permanent residence and later pursue naturalization.

Final application procedures, due-diligence protocols, processing times, and operational rules remain pending.

## Argentina vs Paraguay: What Is the Main Difference?

Paraguay offers an operating SUACE route to permanent residence. Citizenship is a later Supreme Court naturalization, not a result of the investment alone. Specific dollar minimums were not found in Law 6984/2022 and are not stated here.

The Argentine provision is investment naturalization, distinct from temporary or permanent residence. The decrees do not set a confirmed minimum amount. Argentina's pathway should not be treated as an open application program unless the government publishes the applicable criteria and confirms that applications are being accepted.
