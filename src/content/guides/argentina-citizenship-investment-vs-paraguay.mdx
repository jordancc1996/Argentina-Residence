Argentina Citizenship by Investment vs Paraguay Residency by Investment comes down to the goals and priorities of the individual investor. Paraguay's Investor Pass is an operating investment route to permanent residence, while Argentina's proposed program is designed as a pathway to citizenship through a qualifying investment.

Paraguay offers an immediate route to permanent residence, while the Argentina program may appeal more to investors whose primary goal is citizenship rather than residence and who are willing to wait for the program's final rules and application process.

The better option depends on the investor's objective. Someone who wants an available South American residence-by-investment route today may favor Paraguay. Someone specifically seeking citizenship through investment may want to evaluate Argentina once its program becomes operational.

## Argentina vs Paraguay: Which Is Better for Investors?

This is not a ranking of the two countries. Match each program to the outcome you need: a residence permit that can be filed for now, or a citizenship structure that is still waiting on final rules.

### Paraguay may be better for investors who:

* want an operating permanent-residence-by-investment route today
* prioritize immediate availability
* prefer a lower investment threshold depending on the qualifying investment category
* want legal residence in a Mercosur country

Paraguay's Investor Pass, if granted, is permanent residence. It is not citizenship by investment.

### Argentina may be worth considering for investors who:

* specifically want citizenship rather than only permanent residence
* are willing to wait for the program to become operational
* are comfortable with the anticipated higher investment requirements
* place greater value on obtaining nationality and a passport

Argentina is not currently accepting applications. Neither option is universally better.

## What Do I Receive with Paraguay's Investor Pass?

The Investor Pass is permanent residence in Paraguay, not Paraguayan nationality. Successful applicants receive a renewable permanent-residence carnet, reported as valid for ten years, with authorization to work and do business in Paraguay. What you receive is a residence card, not a passport.

Confirm current Investor Pass categories with Paraguayan counsel before treating any brochure as the filing rule. Family members are not automatically included. Current summaries state that dependents apply separately for temporary residence. After two years, the principal may be able to sponsor eligible family members for permanent residence. Argentine family rules have not been published.

## Argentina vs Paraguay Investment Requirements

Argentina vs Paraguay investment requirements are not a single price. Paraguay Residency by Investment is open for residence applications now. Argentina describes a citizenship program that is not open. The amount, what you receive, and whether you can apply are different.

### How much do I need to invest in Paraguay?

Paraguay residency by investment cost depends on the qualifying Investor Pass category. Four tracks are commonly described in summaries of the 2026 ministry rules (MIC Resolution 0283/2026):

- Commercial and industrial investment: a minimum of USD 70,000, creation of at least five formal jobs, and a commercial or industrial plan pre-approved by the Ministry of Industry and Commerce, filed through Paraguay's one-stop investment window (SUACE).
- Stock market holdings are USD 200,000 and are generally subject to a minimum two-year hold.
- Real estate: a minimum of USD 200,000 in acquisition, development, or operation of Paraguayan real estate not intended for personal use.
- Tourism investment: a minimum of USD 150,000 in tourism-related infrastructure, services, or activities.

Those Paraguay Investor Pass investment amounts buy a path to Paraguayan permanent residence, if the file is granted. They do not buy Paraguayan citizenship. Confirm the current official text before treating any brochure amount as the filing rule.

### How much is Argentina expected to cost?

Argentina citizenship by investment cost is anticipated, not a live filing schedule. This site describes two structures, subject to final regulation:

- a $500,000 USD Treasury contribution
- a $1,000,000 USD 7-year government bond

See [investment requirements](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-requirements). Argentina's final operational regulations and application procedures remain pending. Argentina is not currently accepting applications.

A USD 70,000 Paraguay commercial plan with five local hires is not an Argentine Treasury contribution or government bond. An Argentine contribution or bond, when one can be made, does not complete a Paraguay Investor Pass file. Price the residence option and the proposed citizenship option separately.

## Does Paraguay Residency Provide Mercosur Mobility?

No. Paraguay Residency by Investment does not, by itself, provide Mercosur mobility. The Investor Pass makes the holder a foreign permanent resident of Paraguay. It does not make the holder a Paraguayan national.

Mercosur rights that let a citizen of one member country live and work in another follow nationality, not a residence card. That regional mobility is available only if and when Paraguayan naturalization succeeds.

Argentina Citizenship by Investment, if granted, would be Argentine nationality, which is also Mercosur nationality. That is a passport outcome, not a residence-card outcome. This page does not repeat the U.S.-investor Mercosur discussion on [Argentina citizenship by investment for Americans](/research/argentina-citizenship-investment-american-investors).

## Which Option Offers a More Direct Path to Citizenship?

Argentina's proposed route is the more direct citizenship path, if and when it opens: nationality through a qualifying investment, without first completing Paraguay's residence-then-naturalization sequence. Argentina is not currently accepting applications.

Paraguay Residency by Investment is permanent residence first. Naturalization is a later, separate process. Published accounts commonly describe eligibility after three years of legal residence, with Spanish or Guaraní, civic knowledge, and a showing of ties. It is discretionary. Paraguay does not operate citizenship by investment. Paying the Investor Pass threshold does not buy a Paraguayan passport.

A lower Paraguay residence threshold does not shorten that naturalization process. Paraguay residence processing runs on whatever timeline the authorities actually use after a complete file. This site does not publish a Paraguay processing-day table. Argentina's processing time is not yet officially confirmed. See [application process timeline](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-application-timeline). After the April 2026 tender cancellation, Argentine application procedures and due diligence protocols had still not been defined. See the [launch-date research note](/research/argentina-citizenship-by-investment-launch-date).

How an Argentine file would be screened, once one exists, is on the [due diligence process](/guides/argentina-citizenship-investment-due-diligence) guide. Completing a Paraguay Investor Pass review does not create an Argentine file.

## Can Investors Apply to Argentina or Paraguay Now?

Paraguay is processing Investor Pass applications. Capital can be placed in a qualifying Paraguayan commercial plan, tourism project, financial-instrument holding, or non-personal-use real estate, and used to support a permanent-residence file. The result, if granted, is Paraguayan permanent residence. Citizenship remains a later, discretionary naturalization question.

Argentina is not currently accepting applications. Argentina's proposed investment citizenship framework also faces ongoing legal uncertainty, including federal court challenges involving DNU 366/2025. These challenges have not opened or activated the program. The government has not published how applications will be reviewed or where to submit them. Due diligence protocols and a filing portal are still missing. A Paraguayan residence card does not become Argentine nationality.

Paraguay can be filed now for permanent residence. Argentina's citizenship route is not live.
