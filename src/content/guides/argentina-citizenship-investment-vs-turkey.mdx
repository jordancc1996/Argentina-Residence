Argentina citizenship by investment vs Turkey citizenship by investment compares two routes to nationality that are currently at very different stages. Turkey has an operating citizenship-by-investment program, while Argentina's investment citizenship pathway is not yet accepting applications.

[Invest in Türkiye](https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx) states a real-estate route of at least USD 400,000, with a title restriction against resale for three years. Decree 524/2025 does not set an Argentine dollar amount. Reported Argentine figures of $500,000 and $1,000,000 were not found in an enacted regulation.

## Argentina Citizenship by Investment Expected Requirements

Decree 524/2025 is in the Official Gazette and does not set a dollar amount. Secondary reports, which are not an enacted schedule, have described:

- $500,000 Treasury contribution: described as a non-refundable contribution. Not found in the decree.
- $1,000,000 government bond: described as a 7-year bond at 0% interest, with principal returned at maturity. Not found in an enacted regulation.

Those figures are not current qualifying requirements.

A spouse and children under 18 are expected to be eligible for inclusion. Rules for dependent children aged 18 and older remain unknown. See [investment requirements](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-requirements).

A cancelled Argentine consultancy tender described 5,000 APCI recommendation reports or a four-year contract, whichever came first. That is not an application cap. Decree 524/2025 does not require a biometrics visit.

Processing time remains unknown. These anticipated terms should not be treated as final until Argentina publishes the program's implementing rules. For the Argentine program structure independent of this comparison, see the [Argentina Golden Visa Program](/guides/argentina-golden-visa-program) guide.

## Is Argentina Citizenship by Investment Open?

No. Argentina is not currently processing citizenship-by-investment applications.

Argentina established its investment citizenship framework through Decree 524/2025. The Agencia de Programas de Ciudadanía por Inversión (APCI) is responsible for administering the pathway, but the procedures required to submit and process applications have not been finalized.

Investors should therefore distinguish between Argentina's legal framework and an operational program that is ready to accept applications. Official government links are on [Argentina residency resources](/resources).

## How Does Turkey Citizenship by Investment Compare?

<ComparisonBlock id="turkey" />

Turkey is currently processing citizenship-by-investment applications. [Invest in Türkiye](https://www.invest.gov.tr/en/investmentguide/pages/acquiring-property-and-citizenship.aspx) lists these routes, each subject to a presidential decision: at least USD 400,000 in real estate with a three-year resale restriction; at least USD 500,000 in fixed capital; at least 50 jobs; a bank deposit of at least USD 500,000 held for three years; government bonds of at least USD 500,000 held for three years; fund shares of at least USD 500,000 held for three years; or a private-pension contribution of at least USD 500,000 held for three years. The land-registry guide of the [General Directorate of Land Registry and Cadastre](https://yourkeyturkiye.gov.tr/vatandaslik-kazanimi) states the USD 400,000 real-estate threshold for acquisitions from 13 June 2022, and it excludes agricultural land and vacant land acquired after 12 December 2023.

The major difference is availability. Turkey has an operating program. Argentina's expected terms are still awaiting final implementation.

## How Long Does Citizenship by Investment Take?

Turkey's process is generally measured in months. A complete application may realistically take under one year, although timing depends on documentation, valuation, security review, and individual circumstances.

Argentina's processing time is genuinely unknown. Until applications begin and official procedures are published, there is no reliable application-to-citizenship timeline. See [application process timeline](/faq/argentina-citizenship-investment-application-timeline).

## Do You Have to Live in Argentina or Turkey?

Continuous residence is generally not required under Turkey's investment citizenship route, although applicants may need to complete certain procedures in Turkey.

A physical-presence rule for the proposed Argentine framework has not been officially confirmed. Decree 524/2025 does not require a biometrics visit. See [residency requirements](/faq/argentina-residency-physical-presence).

## What Should Investors Know Before Comparing the Programs?

Turkey offers an established citizenship route that can be pursued now. Argentina's investment amounts have not been published as qualifying requirements.

Family rules, residence terms, and a filing calendar have not been published as an enacted Argentine schedule. The 5,000 figure is a cancelled tender quantity, not an application cap.
